PROJECT 01 · LABOUR ECONOMICS

The Participation Puzzle

Female labour force participation remains an important question in India's economic and social development. This project examines why participation rates differ across states.

Executive Summary

Using state-level data from PLFS 2021–22 alongside selected economic, educational and demographic indicators, this study investigates the factors associated with female labour force participation. The analysis uses descriptive statistics, correlation and multiple linear regression in R to assess relationships between participation and factors such as education, state income, urbanisation, non-agricultural employment, fertility and early marriage.

The results are interpreted as associations rather than causal effects. State-level relationships do not necessarily describe individual women's decisions, and the findings must be read alongside the limitations of the data and model.
