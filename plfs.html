PROJECT 01 · LABOUR ECONOMICS

The Participation Puzzle

A state-level study of the factors associated with female labour force participation in India, using data from the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) and other official sources.

Research question

Which state-level economic, demographic and structural factors are associated with female labour force participation in India?

Research approach

The analysis examines relationships between female labour force participation and selected indicators, including education, state income, urbanisation, non-agricultural employment, fertility and early marriage.

Methods

Data cleaning, descriptive analysis, correlation, multiple linear regression and diagnostic checks using R.

Interpretation

The project investigates associations across states rather than causal effects. The findings should be interpreted in light of the sample size, data limitations and the distinction between state-level patterns and individual behaviour.
