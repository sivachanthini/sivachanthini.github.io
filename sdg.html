PROJECT 02 · DEVELOPMENT FORECASTING

Is India on Track for 2030?

A time-series analysis of India's progress towards selected Sustainable Development Goal targets, using historical data and statistical forecasts.

Research question

Based on historical trends, how likely are selected development indicators to reach their 2030 targets?

Indicators examined

  • Maternal mortality ratio
  • Access to safely managed sanitation
  • Access to clean cooking fuels and technologies

Methodology

The project uses historical indicator data and time-series forecasting in R to examine possible trajectories towards 2030. These projections are conditional on historical trends and should not be interpreted as guaranteed outcomes.
