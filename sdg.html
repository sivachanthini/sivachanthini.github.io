PROJECT 02 · DEVELOPMENT FORECASTING

Is India on Track for 2030?

India's progress towards the Sustainable Development Goals raises a question beyond current performance: what do historical trends suggest about the distance still to travel?

Executive Summary

This project uses historical data and time-series forecasting in R to examine India's trajectory on three development indicators: maternal mortality ratio, access to safely managed sanitation, and access to clean cooking fuels and technologies. The analysis projects possible trajectories towards 2030 based on observed historical patterns.

The forecasts are conditional projections, not predictions of guaranteed outcomes. Their interpretation depends on data quality, model assumptions and whether past trends continue. The policy relevance lies in identifying potential gaps between observed trajectories and development ambitions.
