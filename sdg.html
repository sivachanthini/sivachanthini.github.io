PROJECT 02 · DEVELOPMENT FORECASTING

Is India on Track for 2030?

A time-series analysis of India's progress towards selected Sustainable Development Goal targets, using historical data and statistical forecasts.

Research question

Based on historical trends, is India on course to achieve selected development targets by 2030?

Indicators examined

  • Maternal mortality ratio
  • Access to safely managed sanitation
  • Access to clean cooking fuels and technologies

Methodology

Historical trend analysis and time-series forecasting in R to project possible indicator trajectories towards 2030. Forecasts are conditional on historical patterns and should not be interpreted as guaranteed outcomes.

This section will present the principal findings, projected trajectories and implications for India's progress towards the 2030 development agenda.
